🧠 What vearl Actually Does

Most computer networks ask you to trust that the work was done correctly. vearl does the opposite. Every unit of compute that runs through the network leaves behind a proof that it was performed honestly. That proof is checked automatically, and results that do not hold up are discarded.

The "useful work" part matters just as much. Rather than burning electricity on a puzzle with no outcome, vearl directs computing effort toward tasks that produce a real result. Your machine contributes a small share of that effort, and the network assembles those shares into completed work.

From your side of the screen, none of this is complicated. vearl runs quietly in the background, shows you a simple status view, and lets you pause whenever you want. You stay in control the whole time.

⚙️ How It Works

Understanding the flow takes about thirty seconds and makes the rest of the app feel obvious.

  1. vearl connects. When you launch the app, it reaches out to the network and announces that your computer is available.
  2. Work arrives. Small pieces of compute are sent to your machine, sized so they finish quickly.
  3. Your computer processes them. vearl handles this in the background, using a limited share of your processor.
  4. Proofs are attached. Each finished piece of work comes with a proof that it was done correctly.
  5. The network verifies. Proofs are checked. Genuine results are accepted; anything that fails the check is thrown out.
  6. The chain records it. Accepted work is written into the chain, building a permanent, checkable history.

Your job in all of this is to leave vearl running. There is nothing to configure, nothing to tune, and no commands to memorise. The app handles the sequence from start to finish.

💻 System Requirements

vearl is built to run comfortably on ordinary modern hardware. The list below describes a typical, comfortable setup:

  • Operating system: Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • Processor: A dual-core processor from the last several years or better.
  • Memory: 4 GB of RAM, with 8 GB or more giving a smoother experience.
  • Storage: Around 500 MB of free space for the application and its working files.
  • Connection: A stable internet connection, since vearl needs to reach the network.
  • Display: Any standard resolution; the window scales to fit.

If your computer runs a modern web browser without struggling, it will almost certainly run vearl without trouble. Older machines still work but may feel slower while the app is active, in which case pausing when you are not using them is a good habit.

🪄 Your First Run, Step by Step

The first time you open vearl, you will see a short setup screen. Here is what each part means and what to do.

Step one: let it connect

vearl shows a connecting indicator while it reaches the network. This usually takes a few seconds. If it takes longer, check that your internet connection is working and that no firewall is blocking the app.

Step two: choose your power setting

You will be offered a simple choice about how much of your computer vearl may use. The default, balanced option suits most people. If you are on a laptop and want maximum battery life, pick the lighter option. You can change this later at any time.

Step three: decide about startup

vearl asks whether it should open automatically when Windows starts. Turning this on means you never have to remember to launch it. Turning it off means vearl only runs when you open it yourself. Both are reasonable; pick whichever matches how you use your computer.

Step four: watch the status view

Once setup finishes, you land on the main status view. It shows whether vearl is connected, how long it has been running, and a simple feed of recent activity. Leave the window open or minimise it; either way, vearl keeps working.

Good to know: the very first session does a little extra preparation in the background. It may look quiet for a minute or two before activity appears in the log. That is expected behaviour, not a fault.

🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

The download will not start

Refresh the release page and try again. If your browser blocks the download, look for a small icon or message near the address bar and allow the download to proceed. Some browsers hold files until you confirm, so check for a prompt you may have missed.

Windows shows a security prompt

This is standard for software downloaded from the internet. Confirm that you are using the official release page, then choose the option to keep or run the file. If the prompt offers a "more information" link, open it to reveal the option to continue.

vearl opens but stays on "connecting"

First, confirm your internet connection works by opening any website. Second, close vearl fully and open it again. Third, check whether a firewall or security tool is asking for permission and approve it. A restart of your computer resolves the majority of stubborn connection issues.

The app feels slow

Lower the power setting in vearl's preferences so it uses less of your processor. You can also pause vearl while doing something demanding, then resume afterwards. Pausing does not lose your place.

You cannot find the app after installing

Open the Start menu and type vearl. If it does not appear, run the setup again from the official release page and keep the default shortcut option selected.

You want to start over

Pause vearl, close it, and remove it through Windows Settings under Apps. Then download a fresh copy from the official release page and set it up again.
